========================================================
  HUONG DAN CAI DAT & SU DUNG PROMOPILOT AI
  (Danh cho nguoi khong ranh ky thuat - lam theo tung buoc)
========================================================

BUOC 0 - CAI PYTHON (chi lam 1 lan duy nhat, neu may chua co)
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Vao trang: https://www.python.org/downloads/
2. Bam nut tai ban moi nhat (Download Python).
3. Mo file vua tai, o man hinh cai dat dau tien:
   >>> NHO TICK VAO O "Add Python to PATH" <<<
   roi bam "Install Now".
4. Cho cai xong, bam Close.

Neu may da co Python roi thi bo qua buoc nay.


BUOC 1 - GIAI NEN THU MUC
----------------------------------------------------------
Giai nen (unzip) toan bo thu muc "promopilot-ai" ra mot noi
de nho tren may tinh, vi du: Desktop hoac D:\PromoPilotAI

QUAN TRONG: Khong doi ten cac file/thu muc ben trong.


BUOC 2 - CAI DAT LAN DAU (chi lam 1 lan duy nhat)
----------------------------------------------------------
Mo thu muc "promopilot-ai" vua giai nen, TIM va BAM DUP
(double-click) vao file:

    CAI_DAT_LAN_DAU.bat

Mot cua so den (mau xanh la/den) se hien ra va tu chay.
Qua trinh nay can may tinh CO KET NOI INTERNET va co the
mat 2-5 phut. Cu de yen, khong tat cua so, cho den khi thay
dong chu:

    CAI DAT HOAN TAT!

roi bam phim bat ky de dong cua so.

Neu hien loi mau do, chup man hinh lai va gui cho nguoi ho tro.


BUOC 3 - CHAY UNG DUNG (lam moi lan muon dung app)
----------------------------------------------------------
Tu lan nay ve sau, MOI LAN muon dung PromoPilot AI, chi can
BAM DUP vao file:

    CHAY_UNG_DUNG.bat

Cho vai giay, trinh duyet (Chrome/Edge/Cốc Cốc...) se TU DONG
MO ra ung dung. Neu khong tu mo, mo trinh duyet len va go vao
thanh dia chi:

    http://localhost:8501

*** KHONG DONG cua so den trong luc dang dung app. ***
Muon TAT ung dung: dong cua so den do lai, hoac bam Ctrl+C.

LUU Y: Lan chay dau tien app se co san du lieu MAU (demo) de
thu truoc. Muon dung du lieu ban hang THAT cua doanh nghiep,
vao muc "1. Tai du lieu" o thanh menu ben trai trong app, roi
tai len file Excel/CSV ban hang cua ban.


CAU HOI THUONG GAP
----------------------------------------------------------
Q: May bao "khong cho phep chay script" (execution policy)?
A: Mo PowerShell, go lenh sau roi Enter (chi lam 1 lan):
   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope CurrentUser -ExecutionPolicy RemoteSigned
   Sau do thu bam lai file .bat.

Q: Du lieu ban hang cua toi co bi gui len internet khong?
A: KHONG. Ung dung nay chay hoan toan tren may tinh cua ban.
   Du lieu ban hang khong duoc gui di dau ca.

Q: Toi muon dung tren may khac thi lam sao?
A: Copy nguyen ca thu muc "promopilot-ai" sang may do, roi lam
   lai tu Buoc 2 (Cai dat lan dau) tren may moi do.
